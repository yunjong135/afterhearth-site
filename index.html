Support

Contact

Email bornbroke19@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2–3 days. Please include your device model and iOS version.

My save disappeared after an update

Your previous save is backed up automatically. Update to the latest version and reopen the app; it will be restored. If not, email us.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs fully offline.

Download again on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple ID and download Afterhearth again from the App Store at no charge. Save data is stored on the device and does not transfer automatically.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Tap "EN / 한" on the title screen, or use Settings in the pause menu.
